--- layout: publication authors: [Z. Wang, A. Rosentrater,A. Aurandt, C. Johannsen, K. Yvonne Rozier] title: "An Integrated Ecosystem for Mission-time LTL Specification Elicitation and Validation" info: International Symposium on Leveraging Applications of formal methods (ISoLA 2026), October 2026 link: "/publications/pdf/aurandt2026isola.pdf" date: 2026-10-17 00:00:00 tools: [FRET, R2U2] --- Formal methods often stall not on verification algorithms, but on the harder task of turning informal engineering intent into precise, trustworthy requirements. Mission-time Linear Temporal Logic (MLTL) strikes a practical balance for this setting: it is expressive enough to capture many common reactive-system requirements while directly encoding bounded mission-time properties in a form that aligns well with practical engineering workflows. We present https://mltl.temporallogic.org, a zero-install, browser-based ecosystem for MLTL that supports the workflow from initial specification elicitation through validation and downstream verification. The ecosystem supports an iterative loop that starts with a myriad of elicitation methods spanning manual drafting, inference from labeled system traces, translation from structured natural language, and AI-assisted generation, and then feeds into a suite of techniques to help validate that the candidate specification matches the original intent. Using a sounding rocket requirement as a running example, we show how this loop helps engineers move from an informal English requirement to a formal, validated MLTL specification, and then carry it forward into runtime monitoring and model checking for verification. We argue that this kind of integrated, easily accessible tooling is an important research contribution that lowers the practical barrier to formal specification.
